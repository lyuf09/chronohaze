Optimization · Formal Verification · OR for ML

I am a pure mathematics undergraduate mainly interested in problems at the intersection of optimization, formal verification, and machine learning, and in how to organize the structure of those problems clearly.

My current line of work is centered on Operational Research for Machine Learning, and I mainly rely on Isabelle/HOL, optimization theory, and technical writing to make those problems clear.

Research Themes

Research Themes

Optimization / Operations Research

围绕优化、下界、约束结构与 formulation 设计，尤其关注带不变性的问题里，哪些结构是“真的能恢复出来”的。

Formalization in Isabelle/HOL

把近似保证、算法构造与证明组织成可验证、可复用、可扩展的形式化对象，而不是只停留在纸面推导。

Game Theory & Strategic Reasoning

关注多主体决策中的策略互动、信息结构与激励机制，尤其对均衡、动态博弈以及信息不完全情形下的分析方式感兴趣。目前将其作为持续发展的研究兴趣，并逐步整理相关阅读与思考。

AI for Math / OR for ML

我关心 AI 与数学方法之间更结构性的结合方式，也持续沿着 OR for ML 这条线来组织目前的工作。

Optimization / Operations Research

Questions about optimization, lower bounds, constrained structure, and formulation design—especially what can genuinely be recovered once invariances enter the model.

Formalization in Isabelle/HOL

Turning approximation guarantees, algorithmic constructions, and proof structure into machine-checked objects that can be reused and extended.

Game Theory & Strategic Reasoning

I am interested in strategic interaction, information structure, and incentive design in multi-agent decision settings, especially equilibrium analysis, dynamic games, and reasoning under incomplete information. For now, I treat this as a developing research interest and am gradually organizing the related reading and notes.

AI for Math / OR for ML

I am interested in more structural links between AI and mathematical methods, and much of my current work is organized through an OR-for-ML lens.
