THE RESEARCH LINE

结构在被转写之后如何变化？

我的最高层问题是：数学结构在被形式化、对偶化、离散化与算法化之后如何变化。

形式化揭示 theorem dependencies 与可复用接口；对偶与不变性揭示哪些信息仍被保留；离散化则检验哪些约束在计算对象中仍有意义。

正式发表于 AFP（2026）：对基数约束单调子模最大化的 classical greedy 与 verified stateful lazy-greedy 进行形式化。

发表后扩展：当前仓库进一步发展 stochastic-greedy theorem line，包括 sampling、approximation packaging 与 oracle-cost accounting。

THE RESEARCH LINE

What changes when structure is transformed?

My central question is how mathematical structure changes after formalization, dualization, discretization, and algorithmization.

Formalization exposes theorem dependencies and reusable interfaces; duality and invariance reveal which information survives; discretization tests which constraints remain meaningful in computational objects.

Published in AFP (2026): a formalization of the classical greedy algorithm and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization.

Post-publication extension: the current repository additionally develops a stochastic-greedy line, including sampling, approximation packaging, and oracle-cost accounting.
