Academic Landing

学术

优化 · 形式化证明 · OR for ML

我现在是爱丁堡大学数学本科生。我的主要兴趣在优化理论、形式化证明，以及机器学习问题中可以被数学结构化处理的部分。

目前我主要做两条线：一条是把一阶优化算法和近似算法的证明整理成 Isabelle/HOL 中可检查的形式化对象；另一条是围绕非凸优化、minimax 与 localization 问题，写一些技术笔记，尝试把算法直觉、几何结构和可证明的结论连接起来。

这个页面不是完整的 publication list，更像是我目前学术工作的入口：包括已经形式化的项目、正在整理的研究笔记，以及一些还在发展中的问题。

Academic Landing

Academic

A clear entry page: directions, evidence, then notes over time

My current academic work is organized through an OR-for-ML lens, with a recurring interest in making optimization, formal verification, and structured modeling explicit enough to be checked, reused, and extended.

This section is organized around three layers: current research directions, problem framing, and representative work. Readers can begin with the research lines and then move to project materials or technical notes as needed.

Some of these projects are mature formal developments; others are exploratory research notes. I keep both on the site because I care about showing not only final outputs, but also how a problem becomes structured enough to be checked.

研究主题

Research Themes

Direction 01

Optimization and OR

我关心的是优化问题里真正起作用的结构：约束、对偶、下界、局部几何，以及 formulation 本身会保留或丢失什么信息。最近的阅读和笔记主要围绕 nonconvex minimax、localization 和一阶/二阶算法展开。

Direction 02

Formalization in Isabelle/HOL

我使用 Isabelle/HOL 形式化优化算法与近似算法中的证明结构，包括投影梯度法、子模函数贪心算法及其近似保证。相比把结果重新写一遍，我更关心哪些证明模式可以被拆成可复用的组件。

看形式化项目 看相关笔记

Direction 03

AI for Math / OR for ML

我对 AI 和数学之间的关系感兴趣，但更偏向结构化的一面：哪些机器学习问题可以用优化、博弈、OR 或形式化方法重新表达，哪些证明和算法结构可以被更清楚地组织出来。

Direction 01

Optimization and OR

I care about the structures that actually do work inside optimization problems: constraints, duality, lower bounds, local geometry, and what a formulation preserves or loses. My recent reading and notes focus on nonconvex minimax, localization, and first- and second-order algorithms.

Direction 02

Formalization in Isabelle/HOL

I use Isabelle/HOL to formalize proof structures in optimization and approximation algorithms, including projected gradient methods, submodular greedy algorithms, and approximation guarantees. I am especially interested in which proof patterns can become reusable components.

Direction 03

AI for Math / OR for ML

I am interested in the relationship between AI and mathematics, especially on the structural side: which machine learning problems can be re-expressed using optimization, games, OR, or formal methods, and which proof or algorithmic structures can be organized more clearly.

代表性工作

Representative Work

Work 01

Isabelle/HOL: Submodular Greedy Algorithms

形式化 cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization 中的经典 greedy 方法，并整理 LazyGreedy 与 StochasticGreedy 相关证明结构。目前重点是 AFP-facing cleanup、依赖整理与展示收束。

Work Note

Work 02

Isabelle/HOL: First-Order Methods for Smooth Convex Optimization

一个关于 smooth convex optimization 中一阶方法的 Isabelle/HOL 形式化项目，覆盖 gradient descent、projected gradient descent、projected gradient mapping、strong convexity 下的线性收敛等结果。

Work Note

Work 03

TTGDA and Second-Order Tracking

一份关于 TTGDA、value function viewpoint 与二阶 tracking 问题的探索性技术笔记。它不是完成的论文，而是我目前围绕 nonconvex-strongly-concave minimax 问题整理出的一个研究入口。

Notes Note

Work 01

Isabelle/HOL: Submodular Greedy Algorithms

A formalization of classical greedy methods for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization, together with proof structure around LazyGreedy and StochasticGreedy. The current focus is AFP-facing cleanup, dependency organization, and presentation.

Work Note

Work 02

Isabelle/HOL: First-Order Methods for Smooth Convex Optimization

An Isabelle/HOL formalization project for first-order methods in smooth convex optimization, covering gradient descent, projected gradient descent, projected gradient mapping, and linear convergence under strong convexity.

Work Note

Work 03

TTGDA and Second-Order Tracking

An exploratory technical note on TTGDA, the value-function viewpoint, and a second-order tracking question. It is not a finished paper, but a current entry point into my thinking around nonconvex-strongly-concave minimax problems.
