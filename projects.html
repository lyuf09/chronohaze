Selected Work

  1. 01

    PUBLISHED · AFP · 2026

    First-Order Methods for Smooth Convex Optimization in Isabelle/HOL

    Status: Published in the Archive of Formal Proofs on July 2, 2026.

    Problem: Develop reusable Isabelle/HOL infrastructure for smooth convex first-order optimization, with projected-gradient descent as the central application.

    Contribution: Developed the full Isabelle/HOL entry, including projection geometry, descent and telescoping infrastructure, projected-gradient mappings, residual certificates, convex convergence rates, and linear convergence under strong convexity.

    Links:

    AFP Proof document Project note GitHub
  2. 02

    PUBLISHED · AFP · FORMALIZATION · SUPERVISED BY WENDA LI

    Greedy Algorithms for Cardinality-Constrained Submodular Maximization

    Status: Published in the Archive of Formal Proofs on May 26, 2026. The AFP entry covers classical greedy and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant; the current repository additionally contains a post-publication stochastic-greedy extension with the sampling model, approximation proof, and oracle-cost bounds.

    Problem: Formalize the classical 1−1/e approximation guarantee for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization.

    Contribution: I was responsible for the full Isabelle/HOL development under the supervision of Wenda Li.

    Links:

    AFP Proof document GitHub DOI
