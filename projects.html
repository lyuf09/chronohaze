Formal Methods · Submodular Optimization

Isabelle/HOL · Submodular Greedy Guarantees

Machine-checked guarantees for submodular greedy algorithms (Isabelle/HOL) — a modular proof development for classic approximation results.

Technical core:Formalizes monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint and proves the classical (1 − 1/e) guarantee via a reusable gap-recurrence framework.

My contribution:Designed a reusable locale structure, packaged step-spec lemmas, and organized the proof so new greedy variants can reuse the same approximation backbone.

Result / takeaway:The development is structured for extension (e.g., LazyGreedy / StochasticGreedy) without duplicating the approximation argument.

Evidence:repository + theory files + research log

Next:Extend the framework to a stochastic/lazy variant and add a small empirical experiment to demonstrate the oracle-efficiency gain.

Optimization Research

Optimization Theory · Duality / Lower Bounds

Optimization research · Duality that collapses under invariances

When a dual certificate becomes uninformative (and how to fix it) — diagnosing trivial lower bounds under translation invariance.

Technical core:Identifies conditions (e.g., translation invariance / symmetry) under which a standard dual formulation collapses to a constant/trivial bound.

My contribution:Re-derived the dual step-by-step, pinpointed the invariance-driven degeneracy, and proposed structural fixes (e.g., constraint augmentation / gauge-fixing) to recover informative certificates.

Result / takeaway:A clear diagnostic: the issue is structural, not a bug in derivation; meaningful dual signals require breaking the symmetry explicitly.

Evidence:derivation note + annotated steps + meeting summary

Next:Implement and test one “fix” on a benchmark instance and compare bound quality against the collapsed baseline.
