ACADEMIC WORK

Academic Work

  1. 01

    FORMAL VERIFICATION

    Greedy Algorithms for Cardinality-Constrained Submodular Maximization

    Status: Published in AFP, 2026. The AFP entry covers classical greedy and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant; the current repository additionally contains a post-publication stochastic-greedy extension with sampling, approximation packaging, and oracle-cost accounting.

    Question: How can classical and lazy greedy approximation arguments be represented within a reusable Isabelle/HOL framework?

    My contribution: I independently completed the full formalization under the supervision of Wenda Li, including the submodularity infrastructure, approximation argument, and executable/stateful algorithmic components.

    Evidence:

    AFP Proof document GitHub DOI
  2. 02

    FORMAL VERIFICATION

    First-Order Methods for Smooth Convex Optimization in Isabelle/HOL

    Status: Submitted to the Archive of Formal Proofs; awaiting review.

    Question: How can projection geometry, smoothness, convexity, and telescoping arguments be organized as reusable Isabelle/HOL infrastructure for first-order methods?

    My contribution: I independently designed and completed the entire formalization, including the projection inequality, projected-gradient mapping, convex convergence, strongly convex linear convergence, and the public theorem interface. This project had no supervisor.

    Evidence:

    Project page GitHub
  3. 03

    OPTIMIZATION / MINIMAX LEARNING

    TTGDA and Second-Order Tracking

    Status: Working note / exploratory theoretical work.

    Question: Can two-timescale value-function tracking support second-order saddle escaping through the Schur-complement Hessian?

    My contribution: Developed the value-function viewpoint, isolated the second-order tracking gap, and organized a theorem-level route for further verification.

    Evidence:

    Note
  4. 04

    DUALITY / INVARIANCE

    Duality and Structural Scoring under Invariance

    Status: Ongoing theoretical work.

    Question: When translation or gauge invariance collapses a standard dual, can one recover structural certificates tied to localization instance difficulty?

    My contribution: Developed and organized the connection among dual collapse, Range(B), cycle consistency, structural scores, and saddle certificates.

    Research note Derivation history
