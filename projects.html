Dual Degeneration and Structural Recovery under Invariance

A developing set of derivations around dual collapse, Range(B), cycle consistency, and structural score. It comes from my question about why dual lower bounds degenerate in optimization models with translation or gauge invariance, and what structural information survives after that degeneration.

Status:OngoingThe current focus is to stabilize the interpretable part that remains after collapse

Technical Side Project

Metalcore Piano Lab

一个把音频 / MIDI 映射成可玩 chart events 的浏览器原型。它更偏 creative coding，但和我的研究兴趣有相通之处：连续输入如何被离散化成结构化对象，以及哪些约束来自数学结构，哪些来自实际可玩性。

Metalcore Piano Lab

A browser prototype that maps audio / MIDI into playable chart events. It is closer to creative coding, but it connects to my research interests: how continuous input is discretized into structured objects, and which constraints come from mathematical structure versus practical playability.

Status:Work in progressStill adjusting discretization, event mapping, and playability constraints
