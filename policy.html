网站说明（隐私与版权）

最近更新：2026 年 9 月 7 日

1. 数据与隐私

本网站为个人静态展示站点，不提供账号注册、在线支付或用户评论功能。站点本身不主动建立用户画像，也不进行站内广告定向。

网站托管于 GitHub Pages。访问过程中，平台层可能会处理基础网络与日志信息（例如请求日志、IP 地址、设备和浏览器信息等）以保障服务可用性与安全性。具体请参阅 GitHub 官方隐私声明。

2. Analytics、Cookie 与本地存储

本站使用 Google Analytics 了解汇总的页面浏览量、导航模式和设备类别，以便改进网站。Analytics 默认开启，不用于广告、个性化或跨站画像。Google Analytics 可能设置 _ga、_ga_<container-id> 等第一方 Cookie；本站将其有效期设置为最近一次 Analytics 事件后的最多 90 天。Google 会在每次发送 Analytics 命中时更新 Cookie 到期时间，详见 Google 的 cookie_expires 文档。

你可以随时使用全站页脚中的“Turn off analytics”关闭 Analytics。关闭后，此浏览器不会再发送新的 Analytics 事件，相关的可见 Analytics Cookie 会被清除，并在本地保存你的选择。你可以使用同一个控件重新开启 Analytics。浏览器的 Do Not Track 信号也会受到尊重，除非你之后明确重新开启 Analytics。

本站还使用浏览器本地存储保存语言选择（siteLang、chronohaze-lang）、Analytics 偏好（chronohaze-analytics）、搜索界面状态（chronohaze:search-state:v1）和最近搜索词（chronohaze:search-history:v1）。搜索状态与最近搜索词仅保存在此浏览器中，可使用搜索页的“清空记录 / Clear history”删除。临时音频播放状态以 chronohaze:persistent-audio:v1 保存在 sessionStorage 中，通常会在浏览器会话结束时失效。这些存储值用于维持站内设置，不会由本站单独上传。

Google Signals 和广告个性化信号已在代码中关闭。关于 Google 如何处理 Analytics 数据，请参阅 Google Analytics 数据保护说明。

Site Policy (Privacy & Copyright)

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. Data & Privacy

This website is a personal static portfolio. It does not provide account registration, online payment, or user comments. The site does not intentionally build user profiles or run on-site ad targeting.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. During your visit, platform-level infrastructure may process baseline network and log information (such as request logs, IP address, device, and browser data) for service availability and security. Please refer to GitHub's official privacy statement for details.

2. Analytics, Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate page views, navigation patterns, and device categories so I can improve the site. Analytics is enabled by default and is not used for advertising, personalization, or cross-site profiling. Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id>; this site configures them to expire no later than 90 days after the most recent Analytics event. Google updates the expiry whenever an Analytics hit is sent, as described in its cookie_expires documentation.

You can opt out at any time using “Turn off analytics” in the footer. After opting out, no further analytics events will be sent from this browser, visible Analytics cookies will be removed, and your preference will be stored locally. You can re-enable analytics using the same control. A browser-level Do Not Track signal is also respected unless you later choose to turn analytics back on.

The site also uses browser local storage for language preferences (siteLang and chronohaze-lang), the Analytics preference (chronohaze-analytics), search interface state (chronohaze:search-state:v1), and recent search terms (chronohaze:search-history:v1). Search state and recent search terms are stored only in this browser and can be removed using “Clear history.” Temporary audio playback state is stored under chronohaze:persistent-audio:v1 in session storage and normally expires when the browser session ends. These storage values maintain site settings and are not separately uploaded by this site.

Google Signals and advertising-personalization signals are disabled in the site configuration. For information about Google's handling of Analytics data, see Google's Analytics data safeguards.

3. Email Contact

If you contact the site owner by email, your email address and message content are used only for reply and communication, and will not be sold or used for unrelated marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Links

This site may include links to third-party platforms (such as social media or music platforms). After redirection, data and cookies are handled independently by those platforms under their own policies.
