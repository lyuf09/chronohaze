Technical Note · July 2026

Modal Invariants and a Sharp Stability Threshold for Quadratic Symplectic Euler

Feier Lyu

BSc Mathematics (expected 2027), University of Edinburgh

Abstract

This three-page note (plus a one-page reference list) gives a canonical modal reduction of symplectic Euler applied to a positive-definite separable quadratic Hamiltonian. It identifies η2ρ(BC) < 1 as the exact threshold for both absolute convergence of the modified-Hamiltonian series and power-boundedness of the linear symplectic Euler update. It also shows that spectral anisotropy—rather than dimension alone—determines whether the modified Hamiltonian and the elementary quadratic invariant are functionally independent.

Three Core Results

  1. 01 Canonical modal reduction

    A linear canonical transformation separates the positive-definite quadratic dynamics into independent two-dimensional modes, each carrying an explicit quadratic invariant.

  2. 02 One sharp threshold

    The condition η2ρ(BC) < 1 is simultaneously the convergence threshold for the modified-Hamiltonian series and the power-boundedness threshold for the discrete update.

  3. 03 Spectral anisotropy

    Functional independence of the conserved quantities is governed by variation among the modal eigenvalues; an isotropic spectrum keeps the two invariants proportional.
