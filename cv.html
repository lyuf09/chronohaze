Short Summary

I’m currently working on optimization research and formal verification: building reusable Isabelle/HOL developments for classic approximation guarantees, and studying dual/lower-bound formulations that become uninformative under invariances.

Links: research notes, repositories, and selected project artifacts are listed below.

For faculty / research readers, start with the Research landing page (30-second entry: interests, current projects, and notes).

Highlights

  • Formal Methods (Isabelle/HOL): Developing a reusable, well-structured Isabelle/HOL codebase for monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint, including the classical (1 − 1/e) approximation guarantee. (repository + research log available)
  • Optimization Research: Investigating when dual / lower-bound formulations collapse under translation invariance (and related symmetries), and exploring structural fixes (e.g., gauge-fixing / augmentation) that recover meaningful certificates. (notes available)
  • Engineering + IP: Core contributor to an aggregate spreading system designed to reduce material segregation in gravel operations; patents filed (IDs available upon request).
  • Data Analytics: Built and validated retention and funnel analyses from production tables; diagnosed join/identifier issues and aligned metrics with stakeholders and downstream reporting.

Current research directions

  • Submodular optimization: approximation analysis for greedy-type algorithms; proof reuse and modularization for mechanized verification.
  • Duality and certificates: understanding when lower bounds become trivial under invariances, and how to modify formulations to recover informative dual signals.
  • Robust localization models: IRLS-style adaptive weighting frameworks and related first-order methods (reading + early notes).
