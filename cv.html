研究兴趣

我目前主要关注优化、形式化验证与数学建模。更具体地说，我感兴趣的问题包括近似保证、带有不变性的对偶与下界，以及 Isabelle/HOL 中形式化证明的组织方式。

除此之外，我也很在意技术写作本身：怎样把数学或计算工作写得清楚，同时又不损失结构、精确性和真正有用的内容。

代表研究与项目

  1. Isabelle/HOL：子模贪心算法形式化

    我目前正在 Isabelle/HOL 中形式化单调子模最大化以及经典的 (1 - 1/e) 贪心近似保证。这项工作的重点不只是证明单个结论，也包括整理假设、接口和证明组件的组织方式，使后续相关变体能够在同一套结构上继续展开。

  2. 不变性下的对偶与下界

    我一直在研究带图相对结构的距离拟合目标中的对偶与下界问题，尤其关注平移不变性或不可识别性如何导致自然的对偶形式退化为无信息的结果。最近的工作主要集中在弄清这种退化究竟来自哪里，以及加入什么额外结构之后，才能重新得到有意义的下界。

  3. Metalcore Piano Lab

    我做了一个基于浏览器的原型，用来把音频或 MIDI 转换成可玩的节奏游戏谱面事件。这个项目结合了起音与拍点检测、分段、对齐和可玩性约束；同时，我也在记录它的假设、失效模式，以及后续可以如何评估它的效果。

  4. 工程系统：防离析布料系统

    我参与了一个防离析布料系统的建模与关键参数优化，这项工作始终是在真实工况和实际约束下推进的。

Research Interests

My current interests are mainly in optimization, formal verification, and mathematical modeling. In particular, I am interested in approximation guarantees, duality and lower bounds under invariances, and the organization of formal proofs in Isabelle/HOL.

I also care about technical writing: how to present mathematical or computational work clearly, without losing structure, precision, or the actual substance of the work.

Selected Research and Projects

  1. Isabelle/HOL: Formalizing Submodular Greedy

    I am currently formalizing monotone submodular maximization and the classical (1 - 1/e) greedy approximation guarantee in Isabelle/HOL. A large part of the work is not only proving individual statements, but also organizing assumptions, interfaces, and proof components so that related variants can be developed on top of the same structure.

  2. Duality and Lower Bounds under Invariances

    I have been working on duality and lower-bound questions for distance-fitting objectives with graph-relative structure, especially where translation invariance or non-identifiability causes natural dual formulations to collapse. My recent work has focused on understanding exactly where the degeneration comes from, and what additional structure may recover an informative bound.

  3. Metalcore Piano Lab

    I built a browser-based prototype for converting audio or MIDI into playable rhythm-game chart events. The project combines onset and beat detection, segmentation, alignment, and playability constraints, and I have been documenting its assumptions, failure modes, and possible evaluation directions.

  4. Engineering System: Anti-Segregation Spreading

    I participated in modeling and parameter tuning for an anti-segregation spreading system designed for aggregate handling under real deployment constraints.
