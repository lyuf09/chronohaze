Research Directions

Optimization, formal verification, and mathematical modeling; at the moment I am working mainly on submodular greedy guarantees, duality and lower bounds under invariances, and proof organization in Isabelle/HOL.

Selected Projects and Patents

  1. Isabelle/HOL: Formalizing Submodular Greedy

    Object: the classical (1 - 1/e) greedy guarantee for monotone submodular maximization. Result: ongoing Isabelle/HOL formalization and proof-component organization.

  2. Duality and Lower Bounds under Invariances

    Object: graph-relative distance-fitting objectives. Result: diagnosis of lower-bound collapse under invariance and tests of structural fixes.

  3. Metalcore Piano Lab

    Object: discretizing audio / MIDI into playable chart events. Result: a browser prototype with documented pipeline and failure modes.

  4. Engineering System: Anti-Segregation Spreading

    Object: an anti-segregation spreading system. Result: modeling and parameter tuning under real deployment constraints.
