OLFACTORY STUDIES · 2026

Olfactory Notes / 调香手记

Invisible compositions made from tea, wood, flowers, resin, and memory.

气味很难被展示，所以我把它们写成小型的作品说明：一种颜色、一段空气、一个人物的性格，或是一段不想完全说出来的记忆。

01 / METHOD

Scent as Structure

I think of scent as another way of organizing complexity.

A perfume has its own architecture: top notes as the first attack, heart notes as the melodic body, and base notes as the low-frequency memory. In this sense, olfactory work feels unexpectedly close to music production, photography, and proof-writing: each asks what should appear first, what should remain hidden, and what is allowed to stay after everything else fades.
