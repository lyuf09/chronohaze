Project 1

An Ongoing Isabelle Research Project: Formalising Submodular Greedy

Problem: Formalize the classical greedy (1−1/e) approximation guarantee for monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint.

Method: Split the development into constructive and approximation-analysis layers, then encode assumptions and intermediate lemmas explicitly in Isabelle/HOL.
