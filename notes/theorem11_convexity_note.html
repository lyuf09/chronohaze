Convexity in the Necessity Direction of Theorem 11

Feier Lyu · Reading note · Learning Theory / Private Learning / Distributional Constraints · July 2026

This note reads Theorem 11 of Blanchard, Shetty, and Rakhlin, traces its private-learnability necessity direction, records a convexification check for the envelope submeasure and fragmentation numbers, and isolates the bridge from a general distribution class U to the convex setting used in Lemma 18 and Theorem 19.

Source paper

M. Blanchard, A. Shetty, and A. Rakhlin. “Characterizing Online and Private Learnability under Distributional Constraints via Generalized Smoothness.” arXiv:2602.20585, 2026.
