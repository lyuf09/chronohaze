研究记录 · 网络定位 · 2026 年 7 月

一条负残差并不够

从投影预算到负曲率

网络定位研究的 2026 年 7 月阶段记录：应力-刚度分解、多边耦合与依赖环境维数的鞍点。

同一个正五边形构型，在二维空间中可以是一个严格局部极小点（模刚体运动）；将所有点原样补零嵌入三维后，它却会变成一个具有恰好两个负曲率方向的严格鞍点。坐标之间没有发生变化，目标距离也没有改变，改变的只是允许扰动的环境维数。

这个例子来自我最近对网络定位非凸地形的一次重新整理。我最初试图从单条残差为负的边出发构造逃逸方向。对单边目标而言，这种边确实在垂直于当前边的方向上产生负二阶曲率；但网络中的节点扰动由所有相邻边共享，驻点条件又迫使不同边的残差达到力学平衡。局部的负曲率因此不一定能够扩展成整个网络的下降方向。

最先出现的结论反而是否定性的：在一个可微驻点处，恰好一条负残差边永远不足以产生负 Hessian 特征值。真正决定鞍点的是多条负残差边在横向方向上的联合耦合。这篇页面记录这条思路在 2026 年 7 月如何从投影恒等式转向驻点附近的二阶几何。

01 · 起点

这条问题是怎么开始的

最初的问题来自一条对偶下界路线。我希望从 Lagrangian dual 得到一个非平凡的全局下界，用来描述网络定位实例的困难程度。但目标函数在整体平移下不变，普通的对偶计算很容易退化为平凡的零下界。

失败的下界并没有让整条推导失去信息。把边预算投影到可实现的边位移空间 Range(B) 后，仍然留下了一个由测量图和目标距离共同决定的结构量。它最初看起来像一个静态的实例分数，后来却提供了可微驻点上的饱和 witness，并把问题从“能否得到全局下界”引向“驻点附近的二阶几何是什么”。

这条研究路线因此不是从一组预先排好的定理开始，而是从一个没有成功的目标中逐步提炼出仍然稳定的结构。

02 · 稳定结构

已经明确的恒等式

设测量图为 G=(V,E)，节点位置 xi∈ℝd，目标距离 δe≥0。本文只讨论所有测量边当前长度都非零的可微构型。只保留一个目标函数：

F(x) = ∑e=(i,j)∈E (‖xi−xj‖−δe)2.

记 re=‖xi−xj‖，残差 εe=re−δe，归一化应力系数 ae=εe/re。负残差意味着当前边比目标长度短，像一根被压缩的弹簧。

投影边预算

令 PB 表示到 Range(B) 的正交投影，Vδ={ u : ‖ue‖≤2δe }。下面的量是精确的：

Sdual(B,δ) = ∑e∈Eδe2 − ¼ maxu∈Vδ ‖PBu‖2.

Sdual=0 当且仅当存在一个节点构型精确实现全部目标距离。对于森林图，边空间没有环一致性约束，因此这个分数退化为零；对含环图，是否为正还取决于距离数据与环境维数，而不只是图拓扑。

可微驻点 witness 与值恒等式

在可微驻点处，定义逐边饱和的预算向量

ue(x) = 2δe · (Bex / ‖Bex‖).

驻点条件给出 B⊤(2Bx−u(x))=0。由于 2Bx 已经在 Range(B) 中，投影后得到

PBu(x) = 2Bx.

同一个 witness 还精确读出驻点值：

F(x) = ∑e∈Eδe2 − ¼‖PBu(x)‖2.

应力-刚度分解

令 R(x) 为归一化刚度矩阵，Ω(x) 为由系数 ae 组成的应力矩阵。Hessian 可以分成两部分：

½∇2F(x) = Ω(x)⊗Id + R(x)⊤ diag(δe/re) R(x).
几何刚度R⊤diag(δe/re)R 描述沿测量边方向的几何刚性。
残差应力Ω⊗Id 描述残差诱导的横向应力；负残差只可能从这里带来负曲率。

在驻点处，残差并不是独立的，而满足应力平衡 Ω(x)X=0。另一个直接推论是：若归一化刚度矩阵满行秩，驻点必须精确拟合所有距离。特别地，在所有测量边非零时，森林图不存在非精确的可微驻点。

03 · 转折

为什么投影分数不够

Sdual 描述的是实例层面的不相容性：给定的距离预算有多少能够落入可实现边位移空间。它不依赖某个具体驻点，因此也不会记录驻点处的边方向、应力平衡或可行扰动。

同一个 value gap 可以对应完全不同的 Hessian 几何。两个驻点可能具有相同的目标值与相同的 projected-witness gap，其中一个在模刚体运动后是局部极小点，另一个却有负特征值。投影分数因此不能直接区分局部极小点与鞍点。

真正的转折是把注意力从“全局预算保留了多少”移到三个局部问题：哪些边具有负残差；负曲率出现在哪些横向方向；这些边空间方向是否能由共同的节点位移实现。

04 · 单边直觉的失败

为什么一条负残差并不够

单独看一条负残差边，它在垂直于当前边的每个方向上都给出负二阶曲率。但网络中的边扰动不能逐边独立指定：一个节点位移会同时改变所有相邻边，所有边扰动还必须共同落在 Range(B) 中。

单负边结论

在可微驻点处，如果恰好只有一条边具有负残差，而其余边残差非负，那么整体 Hessian 必为半正定。

证明的几何直觉是：驻点的力平衡迫使非负应力网络对唯一的负边做临界补偿，使应力矩阵停留在半正定边界，而第二个刚度项本身半正定。因此“发现一条被压缩的边”并不能推出 strict saddle。

三节点构型已经能够显示界限的 sharpness：当两条负残差边共同出现时，它们的横向作用可以在节点空间中耦合成真正的负曲率方向。核心问题由此从单边筛选变成多边相互作用。

05 · 当前 reduction

正在核对的多边矩阵判据

当前工作稿 · 尚未升级为定理

这一节记录目前正在检查的 reduction。符号约定、kernel/range 条件、伪逆降维，以及如何从小矩阵特征向量恢复节点空间方向仍需逐项核对。

把所有负残差边的横向方向收集到矩阵 C 中，并把对应强度放入 Δ≻0，当前分解写成

½∇2F(x) = P − C⊤ΔC,    P⪰0.

在所需的 kernel/range 条件成立时，候选的横向耦合矩阵为

T⊥ = Δ1/2CP†C⊤Δ1/2.

若当前 reduction 的条件与符号全部通过核对，它将把三个结论压缩到同一个小矩阵中：

∇2F(x)⪰0  ⟺  T⊥⪯I,
ν−(∇2F(x)) = #{ λq(T⊥) > 1 }.

大于 1 的特征向量还应当能够通过 P†C⊤Δ1/2 恢复为节点空间中的负曲率方向。原本需要检查一个 dN×dN Hessian；这个 reduction 的目标，是把决定负惯性的计算降到一个大小至多为 (d−1)|E−| 的矩阵。

目前不把它写成“complete certificate”。网站只记录 reduction 的形状与它需要完成的核对，不把工作稿中的 Weyl 或 effective-resistance 推导作为中心定理。

06 · 例子

正五边形告诉了我们什么

正五边形不是为了展示一段孤立的频谱计算。它同时测试三个问题：逐边标量条件是否足够，多条负边的耦合是否可见，以及环境维数是否会改变同一驻点的类型。

0 < t < 1 二维极小，三维鞍点

在 ℝ2 中，当前计算给出模刚体运动的严格局部极小点；原样补零嵌入 ℝ3 后，新增的横向自由度产生恰好两个负 Hessian 特征值。

1 < t < 3/2 标量测试失效

二维构型已经是鞍点，但逐边有效电阻、最小割与度数条件都检测不到；多边耦合矩阵能够看到联合负曲率。

这个例子说明：逐边条件可以漏掉 collective effect；负残差边之间的相互作用必须放在同一个可实现扰动空间里计算；环境维数不是被动背景，而是优化地形本身的一部分。

07 · 下一步

现在还剩什么问题

当前的矩阵 reduction 试图精确描述负残差边的联合效应，但它仍然依赖驻点处的具体几何。当前工作稿剩下三个问题：

  1. 在最小假设下完成矩阵判据的严谨证明，统一符号并核对 kernel/range 与伪逆条件。
  2. 判断是否存在能够直接控制负惯性指数、同时不丢失多边耦合信息的图结构量。
  3. 把由特征向量给出的存在性方向变成可计算、带下降保证的 escape step。

Research record · Network localization · July 2026

One Negative Residual Is Not Enough

From Projected Budgets to Negative Curvature

A July 2026 research note on stress-rigidity decomposition, multi-edge coupling, and dimension-dependent saddles.

The same regular-pentagon configuration can be a strict local minimum in two dimensions, modulo rigid motions, and become a strict saddle with exactly two negative-curvature directions after the unchanged coordinates are embedded in three dimensions by appending zeros. The target distances do not change. Only the ambient perturbation space does.

This example emerged while I was reorganizing the nonconvex landscape of network localization. I first tried to construct an escape direction from a single edge with negative residual. For one edge, compression does create negative second-order curvature transverse to that edge. In a network, however, node displacements are shared by all incident edges, and stationarity forces the residual stresses to balance. Edgewise negative curvature need not extend to a descent direction for the full system.

The first useful conclusion was therefore negative: at a differentiable stationary point, exactly one negative residual can never produce a negative Hessian eigenvalue. What determines a saddle is the joint transverse coupling of several negative-residual edges. This page records how that question developed during July 2026, from a surviving projection identity to the second-order geometry near stationary points.

01 · Origin

How the question began

I began with a dual lower-bound calculation. The hope was to obtain a nontrivial global lower bound that could say something about the difficulty of a network-localization instance. Translation invariance made the ordinary Lagrangian route collapse easily to the trivial zero bound.

The failed bound did not erase every part of the derivation. After projecting the edge budgets onto the realizable edge-displacement space Range(B), a structural quantity depending on the measurement graph and target distances remained. I first read it as a static instance score. It later supplied a saturated witness at differentiable stationary points and redirected the project from a global bound to local second-order geometry.

The route did not begin with a prepared list of lemmas. It grew by asking what structural information survived a calculation that had failed to meet its original goal.

02 · Stable structure

The identities that remain exact

Let G=(V,E) be the measurement graph, let xi∈ℝd be the node positions, and assume δe≥0. The discussion below stays in the differentiable region where every measured edge currently has nonzero length. There is one objective:

F(x) = ∑e=(i,j)∈E (‖xi−xj‖−δe)2.

Write re=‖xi−xj‖, εe=re−δe, and ae=εe/re. A negative residual means that the current edge is shorter than its target, like a compressed spring.

Projected edge budgets

Let PB be the orthogonal projector onto Range(B) and let Vδ={ u : ‖ue‖≤2δe }. The following quantity is exact:

Sdual(B,δ) = ∑e∈Eδe2 − ¼ maxu∈Vδ ‖PBu‖2.

Sdual=0 if and only if some node configuration exactly realizes every target distance. For a forest, no edge-space cycle constraint remains and the score vanishes. For a cyclic graph, positivity still depends on the distance data and ambient dimension, not on topology alone.

Differentiable stationary witness and value identity

At a differentiable stationary point, define the blockwise saturated budget

ue(x) = 2δe · (Bex / ‖Bex‖).

Stationarity gives B⊤(2Bx−u(x))=0. Since 2Bx already lies in Range(B), projection gives

PBu(x) = 2Bx.

The same witness reads off the stationary value exactly:

F(x) = ∑e∈Eδe2 − ¼‖PBu(x)‖2.

Stress-rigidity decomposition

Let R(x) be the normalized rigidity matrix and let Ω(x) be the stress matrix formed from the coefficients ae. The Hessian separates into two terms:

½∇2F(x) = Ω(x)⊗Id + R(x)⊤ diag(δe/re) R(x).
Geometric rigidityR⊤diag(δe/re)R records rigidity along the measured edge directions.
Residual stressΩ⊗Id records residual-induced transverse stress; negative residuals can introduce negative curvature only through this term.

At a stationary point the residuals are not independent: they satisfy the stress balance Ω(x)X=0. A clean corollary is that if the normalized rigidity matrix has full row rank, stationarity forces every residual to vanish. In particular, when all measured edge vectors are nonzero, a forest has no inexact differentiable stationary point.

03 · The turn

Why the projection score is not enough

Sdual describes instance-level incompatibility: how much of the prescribed distance budget can lie in the realizable edge-displacement space. It does not depend on a particular stationary point, so it does not retain the edge directions, stress balance, or admissible perturbations present there.

The same value gap can accompany different Hessian geometries. Two stationary points can have the same objective value and projected-witness gap while one is a local minimum modulo rigid motions and the other has a negative eigenvalue. The score therefore cannot by itself separate minima from saddles.

The project turned from “how much global budget survives projection?” to three local questions: which edges have negative residual, where their transverse curvature lies, and whether those edge-space directions can be realized by one shared node displacement.

04 · A failed edgewise intuition

Why one negative residual is not enough

Viewed in isolation, a negative-residual edge contributes negative second-order curvature in every direction perpendicular to its current edge vector. But edge perturbations cannot be chosen independently in a network. A node displacement changes every incident edge, and the complete edge perturbation must lie in Range(B).

Single-negative-edge conclusion

At a differentiable stationary point, if exactly one edge has negative residual and all other residuals are nonnegative, then the full Hessian is positive semidefinite.

The proof intuition is mechanical. Force balance makes the nonnegative-stress network compensate the unique negative edge at a critical threshold, leaving the stress matrix on the positive-semidefinite boundary; the rigidity term is already positive semidefinite. Merely finding one compressed edge does not imply a strict saddle.

A three-node configuration shows that the threshold is sharp: two negative residuals can couple through node space and create genuine negative curvature. The central question is therefore multi-edge interaction, not single-edge screening.

05 · Current reduction

The multi-edge matrix criterion under review

Current draft · Not yet promoted to a theorem

This section records the reduction currently being checked. The sign convention, kernel/range assumptions, pseudoinverse step, and recovery of a node-space direction from a reduced eigenvector still require line-by-line verification.

Collect the transverse directions of all negative-residual edges in C, and place their strengths in Δ≻0. The current decomposition has the form

½∇2F(x) = P − C⊤ΔC,    P⪰0.

Under the required kernel/range condition, the candidate transverse coupling matrix is

T⊥ = Δ1/2CP†C⊤Δ1/2.

If the conditions and signs in the current reduction survive verification, three useful statements collapse to this smaller matrix:

∇2F(x)⪰0  ⟺  T⊥⪯I,
ν−(∇2F(x)) = #{ λq(T⊥) > 1 }.

An eigenvector above the threshold should also recover a node-space negative-curvature direction through P†C⊤Δ1/2. Instead of inspecting a dN×dN Hessian, the intended reduction checks a matrix of size at most (d−1)|E−|.

I am not calling this a “complete certificate.” The page records the shape of the reduction and the checks it still needs; the earlier Weyl and effective-resistance calculations remain background motivation rather than public central theorems.

06 · Example

What the regular pentagon reveals

The regular pentagon is not an isolated spectral calculation. It tests three questions at once: whether edgewise scalar conditions suffice, whether multi-edge coupling is visible, and whether ambient dimension can change the type of the same stationary point.

0 < t < 1 A 2D minimum, a 3D saddle

In ℝ2, the current calculation gives a strict local minimum modulo rigid motions. After zero-padding into ℝ3, the new transverse freedom creates exactly two negative Hessian eigenvalues.

1 < t < 3/2 Scalar tests miss the saddle

The configuration is already a saddle in ℝ2, but edgewise effective-resistance, minimum-cut, and degree tests do not detect it; the multi-edge matrix sees the collective negative curvature.

The example shows why edgewise tests can miss collective effects, why several negative edges must be evaluated inside one realizable perturbation space, and why ambient dimension belongs to the optimization landscape rather than serving as passive notation.

07 · What remains

Three questions in the current draft

The current matrix reduction is designed to describe the joint effect of negative-residual edges, but it still depends on the geometry of the particular stationary point. Three questions remain:

  1. Prove the matrix criterion under minimal assumptions, with one consistent sign convention and precise kernel/range and pseudoinverse conditions.
  2. Determine whether graph-structural quantities can control the negative inertia without discarding multi-edge coupling.
  3. Turn the existential direction recovered from an eigenvector into a computable escape step with a quantitative decrease guarantee.
