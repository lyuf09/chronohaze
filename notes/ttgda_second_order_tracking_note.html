Schur-Complement Hessian Tracking in TTGDA

A Note on Possible Second-Order Extensions

Technical note · Optimization / Minimax Learning · June 2026

关于 TTGDA 的技术笔记。这个问题来自我阅读 value-function 视角时的一个想法：如果一阶分析中 y_t 对 y*(x_t) 的 tracking 可以控制梯度误差，那么类似的 tracking 机制是否也能用于 Schur-complement Hessian，从而支持二阶 saddle escaping？

A note on possible second-order extensions of TTGDA: if tracking y_t toward y*(x_t) controls the first-order error in the value-function viewpoint, can an analogous tracking argument control the Schur-complement Hessian well enough for saddle escaping?
