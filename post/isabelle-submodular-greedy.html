早期项目记录：Submodular Greedy 的 Isabelle 形式化

Feier Lyu · Originally posted 2025年12月29日 · 约 4 分钟

Updated 2026年7月 · 保留原始发布日期

2026 年 5 月 26 日这个项目正式进入 AFP，发表范围包括 classical greedy 和 verified stateful lazy greedy。之后仓库还继续做了 StochasticGreedy 的采样、近似证明和 oracle-cost 分析。下面记录的还是 2025 年 12 月的进度，没有把发表后的结果写成当时就已经完成的工作。

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Repository

这篇写于 2025 年 12 月。当时我正在用 Isabelle/HOL 形式化基数约束下单调子模最大化的 greedy approximation theorem。上面的更新记录了后来的 AFP 发表情况，但是下面还是保留当时的进度。

我当时在证明什么

对于单调非负子模函数，在适当的基数约束下 classical greedy 可以得到 1 - 1/e 近似保证。这个结果一般被称为 Nemhauser–Wolsey 的经典贪心近似定理。

纸面证明里经常直接说“选边际增益最大的元素”，然后用子模性得到一个平均增益下界，最后解递推式。但是在 Isabelle 里每一步都要交代对象和条件：集合是不是有限的、贪心选择有没有候选元素、边际增益怎么定义，还有每一步到底用到了哪些单调性和子模性假设。

为什么把构造和分析分开

当时我主要把项目拆成两个 theory：

  • Greedy_Submodular_Construct.thy：定义 greedy sequence、marginal gain 和构造过程里的 invariants。这里只处理算法本身，不先引入近似结论。
  • Greedy_Submodular_Approx.thy：处理平均增益下界、gap recurrence 和最后的 1 - 1/e 近似证明。

我这样拆不是为了让文件看起来整齐，而是因为构造和近似分析需要的条件不完全一样。如果以后要加 lazy greedy 或其他变体，最好能直接复用后面的证明。

写进 Isabelle 后碰到的细节

在纸面上很多推理可以一句话完成，但是写进 Isabelle 的时候就得把每一步拆开。比如集合增加一个元素以后哪些性质还成立，或者那条看起来很直接的不等式具体用了哪个假设，都要核对。

这也是为什么我开始更在意 lemma 的组织方式。如果一个小条件被藏在很长的证明里，后面要复用时还得重新找出来。

2025 年 12 月的进度

当时已经有了基本的 greedy construction、边际增益的结构引理和近似证明需要的递推框架。

还在继续处理的包括子模性假设的模块化、几个技术引理的整理，以及以后做具体实例化需要的接口。

接下来计划尝试用 locale 抽象常用条件，加入 coverage function 这样的实例，并检查可执行实验和其他 greedy 变体能不能接进来。这些是当时的计划，后续成果以上面的发表更新和仓库记录为准。

Early Project Note: Formalising Submodular Greedy

Feier Lyu · Originally posted 29 December 2025 · ~4 min read

Updated July 2026 · original posting date retained

Published in AFP on May 26, 2026: the entry covers classical greedy and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant. Post-publication extension: the repository later added StochasticGreedy sampling, approximation proofs and oracle-cost analysis. The text below describes where the project stood in December 2025. It does not claim the later work was already complete.

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Repository

I wrote this note in December 2025, when I was formalising the greedy approximation theorem for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximisation in Isabelle/HOL. The update above records its later publication in AFP, but the main text keeps the progress as it was at the time.

What I was trying to prove

For a monotone non-negative submodular function, classical greedy has a 1 - 1/e approximation guarantee under the appropriate cardinality constraint. This is the classical Nemhauser–Wolsey greedy approximation result.

On paper, you can often just write “choose the element with the largest marginal gain”, use submodularity to get an average-gain bound, then solve the recurrence. But in Isabelle I need to state the objects and assumptions for every step. Is the ground set finite? Are there any candidates left? How is marginal gain defined? Which steps need monotonicity, and which need submodularity?

Why I separated the construction and analysis

At that stage I divided the project into two main theory files:

  • Greedy_Submodular_Construct.thy: Defines the greedy sequence, marginal gains and invariants of the construction. It describes the algorithm without introducing the approximation bound.
  • Greedy_Submodular_Approx.thy: Contains the average-gain bound, gap recurrence and final 1 - 1/e approximation proof.

I didn't separate them just to make the files look neat. Constructing a greedy sequence and analysing its approximation guarantee need different assumptions. If I later add lazy greedy or another variant, I'd like to reuse the approximation proof where possible.

Details that needed more care in Isabelle

Some arguments that take one sentence on paper have to be split into several steps in Isabelle. For example, I have to check which properties are preserved when adding an element to a set, and exactly which assumption gives an inequality that looked obvious in the handwritten proof.

That's also why I started paying more attention to the way I organise lemmas. If a useful condition is buried inside a long proof, I have to find it again when I want to reuse it.

Progress in December 2025

At the time I had the basic greedy construction, lemmas about marginal gains and the recurrence needed for the approximation proof.

I was still working on separating out the submodularity assumptions, reorganising a few technical lemmas and making the definitions easier to use in concrete examples.

My next plans were to use locales for common assumptions, add examples such as coverage functions, and see whether executable experiments or other greedy variants could fit into the project. Those were my plans at the time. The publication update and repository record show what happened later.
