研究

优化 · 形式化验证（Isabelle/HOL） · 数学建模

我是一名数学本科生，目前主要在几条彼此相关的方向上工作：一是对子模贪心算法近似保证的形式化验证；二是研究带有不变性的优化模型中，对偶与下界为什么会失去信息，以及怎样把这种信息恢复回来；三是做一些小型计算原型，把连续信号转成离散结构，并把其中的建模假设和失效模式写出来。

这里先给出一个简要概览；更长的技术笔记、仓库和项目材料都在下方链接中。

Research

Optimization · Formal Verification (Isabelle/HOL) · Mathematical Modeling

I am an undergraduate mathematics student currently working across several connected directions: formal verification of approximation guarantees for submodular greedy algorithms, duality and lower-bound questions in optimization models with invariances, and small computational prototypes that turn continuous signals into discrete structures while keeping track of their modeling assumptions and failure modes.

This page gives a concise overview of what I am currently working on. Longer technical notes, repositories, and project materials are linked below.

研究兴趣

  • 优化
    我关注对偶、下界、一阶结构与数学建模，尤其在平移不变性、对称性或可识别性问题会直接影响模型表达能力的时候，这类问题会更吸引我。
  • 形式化验证
    我使用 Isabelle/HOL 形式化数学论证。除了把定理证出来，我也关心假设怎样打包、lemma 怎样复用，以及一个 development 如何支持后续变体，而不只停在单个结论上。
  • 子模算法
    我尤其关注单调子模最大化中的贪心算法、它们的近似保证，以及这些近似分析如何在机器检验环境下被组织成清晰、可复用的形式。
  • 建模与结构
    更广义地说，我对这样的问题很感兴趣：一个数学模型在真正面对对称性、不变性、近似需求或实现约束时，会发生怎样的结构变化。

Research Interests

  • Optimization
    I am interested in duality, lower bounds, first-order structure, and mathematical modeling, especially when invariances or identifiability issues affect what can actually be recovered from a formulation.
  • Formal Verification
    I use Isabelle/HOL to formalize mathematical arguments. Besides proving the theorem itself, I care about how assumptions are packaged, how lemmas are reused, and how a development can support later variants instead of stopping at one statement.
  • Submodular Algorithms
    I am especially interested in greedy algorithms for monotone submodular maximization, their approximation guarantees, and how those guarantees can be organized formally in a machine-checked setting.
  • Modeling and Structure
    More broadly, I am interested in how a mathematical formulation changes once it is forced to face symmetry, invariance, approximation, or implementation constraints.

当前工作

Current Work

Isabelle/HOL：子模贪心算法形式化

问题： 在 Isabelle/HOL 中形式化验证：单调子模最大化在基数约束下，贪心算法满足经典的 (1 - 1/e) 近似保证。

方法： 使用 Isabelle/HOL、基于 locale 的组织方式、分步证明结构，以及把贪心步骤和近似递推尽量分开处理。

目前做了什么： 我正在搭建主要的形式化证明，同时也在反复调整假设、lemma 与接口的边界，看看哪些部分能够在相关贪心变体里直接沿用。

当前状态： 进行中。

不变性下的对偶与下界

问题： 对于某些由图上的相对差分定义出的距离拟合目标，自然写出的对偶形式会因为平移不变性或相关的不可识别性而退化，最终只能给出无信息的下界。

方法： 比较 Fenchel/DC 与 Lagrangian 两条路线，并逐步检查：是哪个不变性让消元后的下界变成平凡结果，以及加入什么约束后情况会改变。

目前做了什么： 我已经把几条主要推导路线重新走通，也比较清楚地看到平凡下界是在哪一步出现的。下一步会继续测试 gauge-fixing、增广或更紧约束是否足以恢复非平凡下界。

当前状态： 进行中。

Isabelle/HOL: Formalizing Submodular Greedy

Problem: Formally verify the classical greedy (1 - 1/e) approximation guarantee for monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint.

Methods: Isabelle/HOL, locale-based organization, stepwise proof structure, and a split between the greedy step and the approximation recurrence.

What I have done so far: I have been building the main formal development while also adjusting the boundary between assumptions, lemmas, and interfaces so that closely related greedy variants do not have to start over.

Current status: Ongoing.

Note Projects Repo

Duality and Lower Bounds under Invariances

Problem: For certain distance-fitting objectives defined through graph-relative differences, natural dual formulations may collapse to trivial constant bounds because of translation invariance or related non-identifiability.

Methods: Comparison of Fenchel/DC and Lagrangian routes, with attention to exactly which invariance causes the collapse and what added constraint changes the picture.

What I have done so far: I have worked through the main derivations, identified where the trivial bound appears, and narrowed the next step to gauge-fixing, augmentation, or tighter constraints.

Current status: Ongoing.

Metalcore Piano Lab | Audio / MIDI to Chart (WIP)

Problem: Map continuous audio or MIDI input into playable rhythm-game chart events. At its core, this is a constrained discretization problem.

Methods: Onset detection, beat tracking, segmentation, alignment, and playability constraints.

What I have done so far: I built a browser-based prototype and documented the main pipeline, including its assumptions, limitations, and the questions I want to evaluate more carefully next.

Current status: Work in progress.

最近在想的问题

Current Questions

  • 一个下界究竟是在什么时候因为不变性而失去信息？又需要多小的结构修正，才足以把有用的信息恢复回来？
  • 怎样组织 Isabelle development，才能让它不只是证明一个定理，而是真的能够比较自然地支持后续变体？
  • 当离散化输出既要可玩、可理解，又不能只是“算法上勉强成立”时，应该如何评估它的质量？
  • When does a lower bound become trivial because of invariance, and what is the smallest structural change that restores useful information?
  • How can an Isabelle development be organized so that it scales beyond a single theorem and supports later variants naturally?
  • How should discretization quality be evaluated when the output is intended to be playable, interpretable, and not merely algorithmically admissible?
