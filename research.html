Feier Lyu（Fay Lyu）

我研究优化理论与形式化验证。目前的项目包括：（i）面向子模最大化与投影一阶方法的可复用 Isabelle/HOL 基础设施；（ii）与 Shoham Sabach 合作研究网络定位中的负曲率结构证书。

Research Statement

Feier Lyu (Fay Lyu)

I work on optimization theory and formal verification. My current projects are: (i) reusable Isabelle/HOL infrastructure for submodular maximization and projected first-order methods; and (ii) structural certificates for negative curvature in network localization, in collaboration with Shoham Sabach.

当前正在解决的三个问题

Three current questions

  1. 01

    如何把 classical greedy、stateful lazy-greedy 与 stochastic-greedy 的假设、状态不变量和近似保证组织成可复用的 Isabelle/HOL 接口？

  2. 02

    如何用一组稳定的 projection geometry 与 projected-gradient mapping 引理，同时支持凸收敛和强凸线性收敛率？

  3. 03

    对网络定位中的非最优驻点，哪些图结构与几何条件能给出可计算的负曲率证书和局部逃逸方向？

  1. 01

    How can the assumptions, state invariants, and approximation guarantees for classical greedy, stateful lazy-greedy, and stochastic-greedy be exposed through reusable Isabelle/HOL interfaces?

  2. 02

    Which reusable projection-geometry and projected-gradient-mapping lemmas support both convex convergence and a strongly convex linear rate?

  3. 03

    For non-optimal stationary points in network localization, which graph and geometric conditions yield a computable negative-curvature certificate and a local escape direction?

主要研究方向

Primary Research Directions

PRIMARY DIRECTION 01

机器检查优化与可复用证明结构

这条研究线把算法假设、状态不变量、关键不等式与收敛结论拆成可复用的 Isabelle/HOL 组件。

2026年5月26日正式发表于 AFP：对基数约束下的单调子模最大化，形式化 classical greedy algorithm 与 verified stateful lazy-greedy variant。发表后扩展：当前仓库还进一步发展 StochasticGreedy theorem line，包括 sampling、approximation packaging 与 oracle-cost accounting。关于 smooth convex optimization 与 projected gradient descent 的第二个形式化项目已于2026年7月2日提交至 Archive of Formal Proofs，目前等待审核。

当前重点是减少不同算法证明之间重复的 projection、telescoping 与 approximation bookkeeping。

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Proof document Repository

PRIMARY DIRECTION 02

网络定位中的负曲率证书

与 Shoham Sabach 的合作研究图结构如何控制非最优驻点附近的 Hessian 负曲率。

当前目标是从 cycle consistency、projected edge budgets 与图的环空间中构造可验证的局部逃逸方向，并明确这些证书成立所需的几何假设。

PRIMARY DIRECTION 01

Machine-checked optimization and reusable proof structure

This line separates algorithmic assumptions, state invariants, key inequalities, and convergence statements into reusable Isabelle/HOL components.

Published in AFP on May 26, 2026: a formalization of the classical greedy algorithm and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization. Post-publication extension: the current repository additionally develops a stochastic-greedy line, including sampling, approximation packaging, and oracle-cost accounting. A second formalization project on smooth convex optimization and projected gradient descent was submitted to the Archive of Formal Proofs on July 2, 2026 and is awaiting review.

The current goal is to reduce repeated projection, telescoping, and approximation bookkeeping across different algorithm proofs.

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Proof document Repository

PRIMARY DIRECTION 02

Negative-curvature certificates in network localization

In collaboration with Shoham Sabach, I study how graph structure controls Hessian negative curvature near non-optimal stationary points.

The current goal is to construct verifiable local escape directions from cycle consistency, projected edge budgets, and the graph cycle space, while making the required geometric assumptions explicit.
