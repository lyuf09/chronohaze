研究陈述

Feier Lyu（Fay Lyu）

我的工作关注结构性假设如何决定优化算法的保证。在 Isabelle/HOL 中，我研究如何把状态不变量与几何引理组织成可复用的证明接口；在网络定位中，我研究图结构与应力结构如何控制非最优驻点附近的负曲率。这些项目共享同一个目标：让算法保证背后的数学依赖足够明确，从而能够被分析、验证与复用。

Research Statement

Feier Lyu (Fay Lyu)

My work asks how structural assumptions determine the guarantees of optimization algorithms. In Isabelle/HOL, I study how state invariants and geometric lemmas can be organized into reusable proof interfaces; in network localization, I study how graph and stress structure govern negative curvature near non-optimal stationary points. These projects share a common aim: to make the mathematical dependencies behind algorithmic guarantees explicit enough to analyze, verify, and reuse.

当前正在解决的三个问题

Three current questions

  1. 01

    如何把经典贪心、有状态惰性贪心与 StochasticGreedy 的假设、状态不变量和近似保证组织成可复用的 Isabelle/HOL 接口？

  2. 02

    如何在统一的概率运行语义中结合期望近似保证与可执行轨迹层面的预言机调用复杂度保证？

  3. 03

    对网络定位中的非最优驻点，哪些图结构与几何条件能给出可计算的负曲率证书和局部逃逸方向？

  1. 01

    How can the assumptions, state invariants, and approximation guarantees for classical greedy, stateful lazy-greedy, and stochastic-greedy be exposed through reusable Isabelle/HOL interfaces?

  2. 02

    How can the expected approximation guarantee and the executable trace-level oracle-cost guarantee be unified within a single probabilistic run semantics?

  3. 03

    For non-optimal stationary points in network localization, which graph and geometric conditions yield a computable negative-curvature certificate and a local escape direction?

主要研究方向

Primary Research Directions

主要方向 01

机器检查优化与可复用证明结构

这条研究线把算法假设、状态不变量、关键不等式与收敛结论拆成可复用的 Isabelle/HOL 组件。

2026年5月26日正式发表于 AFP：对基数约束下的单调子模最大化，形式化经典贪心算法与经验证的有状态惰性贪心变体。发表后，当前仓库继续发展 StochasticGreedy 定理线，包括采样模型、近似证明与预言机调用复杂度界。关于光滑凸优化与投影梯度下降的第二个形式化项目已于2026年7月2日提交至 Archive of Formal Proofs，目前等待审核。

当前重点是减少不同算法证明之间重复的投影、望远镜求和与近似保证整理工作。

AFP 条目 DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 证明文档 代码仓库

主要方向 02

网络定位中的负曲率证书

与 Prof. Shoham Sabach 的合作研究图结构如何控制非最优驻点附近的 Hessian 负曲率。

当前目标是从环一致性、投影边预算与图的环空间中构造可验证的局部逃逸方向，并明确这些证书成立所需的几何假设。

PRIMARY DIRECTION 01

Machine-checked optimization and reusable proof structure

This line separates algorithmic assumptions, state invariants, key inequalities, and convergence statements into reusable Isabelle/HOL components.

Published in AFP on May 26, 2026: a formalization of the classical greedy algorithm and a verified stateful lazy-greedy variant for cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization. Post-publication extension: the current repository additionally develops a stochastic-greedy line, including the sampling model, approximation proof, and oracle-cost bounds. A second formalization project on smooth convex optimization and projected gradient descent was submitted to the Archive of Formal Proofs on July 2, 2026 and is awaiting review.

The current goal is to reduce repeated projection, telescoping, and approximation bookkeeping across different algorithm proofs.

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Proof document Repository

PRIMARY DIRECTION 02

Negative-curvature certificates in network localization

In collaboration with Prof. Shoham Sabach, I study how graph structure controls Hessian negative curvature near non-optimal stationary points.

The current goal is to construct verifiable local escape directions from cycle consistency, projected edge budgets, and the graph cycle space, while making the required geometric assumptions explicit.
