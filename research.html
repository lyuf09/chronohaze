Formal Methods · Submodular Optimization

An Ongoing Isabelle Research Project: Formalising Submodular Greedy

Problem: Machine-check the classical greedy (1−1/e) guarantee for monotone submodular maximization under a cardinality constraint.

Method: Build a reusable Isabelle/HOL development (locales + step-spec interfaces), separating constructive algorithm structure from approximation analysis.

Contribution: A proof skeleton that scales to variants (e.g., LazyGreedy/StochasticGreedy) and keeps assumptions explicit and reusable.

Status: Active

Optimization Research · Duality / Lower Bounds

Spring 2026 | Duality under Invariances (Cornell ORIE)

Problem: Find nontrivial lower bounds for a graph-relative-difference distance-fitting objective where natural formulations can collapse due to translation invariance / non-identifiability.

Method: Diagnose degeneracy first (what makes the dual trivial), then add structure (gauge-fixing / tighter constraints / augmentation) before choosing a dualization route (Fenchel/DC vs. Lagrangian).

Contribution: A structure-first roadmap: when/why bounds collapse, plus candidate fixes that restore informative dual signals.

Status: Active

Signal / Discretization Prototype · Data-oriented Systems

Metalcore Piano Lab | From Audio to Chart (WIP)

Problem: Map continuous audio (or MIDI) into a playable rhythm-game chart as a discretization problem with constraints.

Method: Onset/beat detection, tempo tracking, segmentation/alignment, then playability-oriented constraints and evaluation targets.

Contribution: A browser-native prototype + notes that document assumptions, failure modes, and next-step heuristics.

What I’m Currently Thinking About

Questions and themes I’m actively using to steer reading, proof work, and modeling choices.

  • How to restore informative lower bounds under invariances: gauge-fixing vs. augmentation (e.g., augmented Lagrangian / ADMM-style structure).
  • How to make Isabelle proofs scale: interface design (locales), step-spec patterns, and reusable lemma libraries.
  • When a relaxation is “too loose”: minimal structural constraints that recover signal without breaking tractability.
  • How to evaluate discretization quality: objective proxies + constraint design for playability and interpretability.
