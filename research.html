研究

我现在的研究兴趣主要围绕优化理论、形式化证明，以及数学结构在算法与机器学习问题中的表达方式。

相比把这些方向看成互不相关的兴趣点，我更关心一个共同的问题：当一个数学对象被形式化、对偶化、离散化或算法化之后，哪些结构仍然被保留下来，哪些信息会在重写过程中丢失？

目前我主要沿着三条线推进：一是用 Isabelle/HOL 整理优化与近似算法中的证明结构；二是研究带有不变性的优化模型中，对偶、下界与结构恢复之间的关系；三是通过一些原型系统理解连续对象到离散结构的转换。

Research

My current research interests center on optimization theory, formal proof, and the ways mathematical structure is expressed in algorithms and machine-learning problems.

Rather than treating these as separate interests, I am interested in one shared question: when a mathematical object is formalized, dualized, discretized, or algorithmized, which structures are preserved and which information is lost in the rewriting?

At the moment I am moving along three lines: using Isabelle/HOL to organize proof structures in optimization and approximation algorithms; studying duality, lower bounds, and structural recovery in optimization models with invariance; and using prototype systems to understand the passage from continuous objects to discrete structure.

研究线索

Research Lines

Isabelle/HOL 与优化算法形式化

我正在使用 Isabelle/HOL 形式化一阶优化方法与子模贪心算法中的经典证明结构。这里我关心的不只是把定理放进 proof assistant，而是如何把证明中的依赖、接口和可复用部分写清楚。

目前，关于 cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization 的 greedy、LazyGreedy 与 StochasticGreedy 证明线已经基本成形；另一条关于 smooth convex optimization 与 projected gradient descent 的形式化项目也在整理中。

接下来我想继续思考的是：怎样把这些形式化结果整理成更稳定的 library，使它们既适合展示和归档，也能支持后续扩展。

不变性、对偶与结构恢复

在一些优化模型中，最自然的对偶路线会因为 translation 或 gauge invariance 而退化，最后只得到 trivial lower bound。我感兴趣的是：这种退化到底说明了推导失败，还是说明模型本身隐藏着另一种结构？

我目前正在重新整理相关推导，尤其关注 Range(B)、cycle consistency 与 instance difficulty 之间的关系。对我来说，这条线的核心不是单纯追求一个更强的 bound，而是理解在对偶信息 collapse 之后，是否还可以恢复出可解释、可比较的 structural score。

Prototype / discretization

这条线来自一些音乐与交互原型项目，例如把音频、节奏或事件流转成可操作的离散对象。它表面上更像 creative coding，但我关心的问题其实和前两条一致：一个连续对象被离散化之后，哪些结构必须保留，哪些约束只是实现层面的近似？

目前我更适合把它放在技术项目或 side project 里，而不是和 Isabelle/HOL、optimization theory 完全并列为主研究方向。

Isabelle/HOL and Formalization of Optimization Algorithms

I am using Isabelle/HOL to formalize classical proof structures in first-order optimization methods and submodular greedy algorithms. I care not only about putting theorems into a proof assistant, but about making dependencies, interfaces, and reusable proof components explicit.

The proof line for greedy, LazyGreedy, and StochasticGreedy in cardinality-constrained monotone submodular maximization is largely in place; another formalization project around smooth convex optimization and projected gradient descent is also being organized.

The next question for me is how to turn these developments into a more stable library that is suitable for presentation and archiving while still supporting later extensions.

Invariance, Duality, and Structural Recovery

In some optimization models, the most natural dual routes degenerate because of translation or gauge invariance and only produce a trivial lower bound. I am interested in whether that degeneration means the derivation failed, or whether the model is hiding another kind of structure.

I am currently reorganizing the related derivations, especially the relation among Range(B), cycle consistency, and instance difficulty. The point is not only to obtain a stronger bound, but to understand whether interpretable and comparable structural scores remain after dual information collapses.

Prototype / discretization

This line comes from music and interaction prototypes, such as turning audio, rhythm, or event streams into operable discrete objects. On the surface it is closer to creative coding, but the question is continuous with the other lines: after a continuous object is discretized, which structures must be preserved and which constraints are only implementation-level approximations?

For now I think this belongs more naturally as a technical side project rather than as a main research direction on equal footing with Isabelle/HOL and optimization theory.

为什么这些方向连在一起

Why these lines belong together

这些方向看起来分散，但它们对我来说都在问同一个问题：当我们把一个对象改写成另一种形式时，结构到底发生了什么。

Isabelle/HOL 迫使证明结构变得明确；对偶与不变性问题迫使模型结构变得明确；prototype / discretization 则把类似的问题放回具体输入中，检验我们保留下来的结构是否真的有用。

These directions may look scattered, but for me they ask the same question: when we rewrite an object into another form, what happens to its structure?

Isabelle/HOL forces proof structure to become explicit; duality and invariance force model structure to become explicit; prototype / discretization returns the same issue to concrete inputs and tests whether the preserved structure is actually useful.
