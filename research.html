研究陈述

这一页只回答一个问题：我现在怎样理解这些问题。

我目前的学术兴趣集中在优化理论、形式化验证，以及 OR for ML 中出现的结构化建模问题。现阶段，我把机器可检查的优化证明，以及不变性下的对偶与结构证书视为两条主要研究线。

我还不把这些兴趣描述成一个已经完全成型的研究体系。更准确地说，我反复遇到的是同一类问题：当一个数学对象被形式化、对偶化或转写为计算对象时，哪些结构被保留下来，哪些信息会消失？原型与离散化工作则是辅助性的探索实验，用来在具体对象中寻找和检验这些结构。

Research Statement

This page addresses one question: how I currently understand these problems.

My current academic interests are in optimization theory, formal verification, and structured modeling problems arising in OR for ML. At present, I see machine-checkable optimization proofs and duality under invariance as the two main research lines.

I do not yet regard these interests as a fully formed research program. The recurring question is more modest: when a mathematical object is formalized, dualized, or rewritten as a computational object, which structures are preserved and which information is lost? Prototypes and discretization serve as supporting exploratory experiments for finding and testing such structure in concrete settings.

主要研究线与探索性实验

Main Research Lines and Exploratory Work

主线一：机器可检查的优化证明

我正在使用 Isabelle/HOL 形式化一阶优化方法与子模贪心算法中的经典证明结构。这里我关心的不只是把定理放进 proof assistant，而是如何把证明中的依赖、接口和可复用部分写清楚。

其中，Greedy Algorithms for Cardinality-Constrained Submodular Maximization 已正式发表于 Archive of Formal Proofs，发表范围包括 classical greedy 的 1−1/e 近似保证与 verified stateful lazy greedy variant。StochasticGreedy 仍是 future extension / experimental branch；关于 smooth convex optimization 与 projected gradient descent 的第二个形式化项目已提交至 Archive of Formal Proofs，目前等待审核。

接下来我想继续思考的是：怎样把这些形式化结果整理成更稳定的 library，使它们既适合展示和归档，也能支持后续扩展。

主线二：对偶、不变性与结构证书

在一些优化模型中，最自然的对偶路线会因为 translation 或 gauge invariance 而退化，最后只得到 trivial lower bound。我感兴趣的是：这种退化到底说明了推导失败，还是说明模型本身隐藏着另一种结构？

我目前正在重新整理相关推导，尤其关注 Range(B)、cycle consistency 与 instance difficulty 之间的关系。对我来说，这条线的核心不是单纯追求一个更强的 bound，而是理解在对偶信息 collapse 之后，是否还可以恢复出可解释、可比较的 structural score。

探索性实验：原型、离散化与结构寻找

这类原型工作来自音乐与交互系统，例如把 audio / rhythm streams 转换为可检查的离散对象，并观察连续输入被离散化时哪些约束仍然有效。

这还不是一条成熟的研究线，而是我用来检验结构性约束如何作用于具体计算对象的一种技术实验。

Line 1. Machine-checkable optimization proofs

I am using Isabelle/HOL to formalize classical proof structures in first-order optimization methods and submodular greedy algorithms. I care not only about putting theorems into a proof assistant, but about making dependencies, interfaces, and reusable proof components explicit.

Greedy Algorithms for Cardinality-Constrained Submodular Maximization is published in the Archive of Formal Proofs, covering the classical greedy 1−1/e approximation guarantee and a verified stateful lazy greedy variant. StochasticGreedy remains a future extension / experimental branch; a second formalization project on smooth convex optimization and projected gradient descent has been submitted to the Archive of Formal Proofs and is awaiting review.

The next question for me is how to turn these developments into a more stable library that is suitable for presentation and archiving while still supporting later extensions.

AFP entry DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21054718 Proof document Repository

Line 2. Duality, invariance, and structural certificates

In some optimization models, the most natural dual routes degenerate because of translation or gauge invariance and only produce a trivial lower bound. I am interested in whether that degeneration means the derivation failed, or whether the model is hiding another kind of structure.

I am currently reorganizing the related derivations, especially the relation among Range(B), cycle consistency, and instance difficulty. The point is not only to obtain a stronger bound, but to understand whether interpretable and comparable structural scores remain after dual information collapses.

Side prototype. Prototypes and discretization as exploratory structure-finding

These prototypes arise from music and interactive systems—for example, turning audio / rhythm streams into inspectable discrete objects and observing which constraints survive discretization.

This is not yet a mature research line, but a way for me to test how structural constraints behave in concrete computational objects.

为什么这些方向连在一起

Why these lines belong together

前两条是我目前希望继续深入的学术主线：Isabelle/HOL 迫使证明中的依赖与接口变得明确；对偶与不变性问题则迫使模型中的不可辨识性、下界与结构证书变得明确。

原型与离散化不与它们并列为同等成熟的研究方向。它更像一个侧面的实验场：把抽象约束放进具体计算对象里，看哪些结构真的能被保留和使用。

The first two are the academic lines I currently want to pursue further. Isabelle/HOL makes proof dependencies and interfaces explicit; duality under invariance makes non-identifiability, lower bounds, and structural certificates explicit.

Prototypes and discretization are not presented as an equally mature third line. They are a side laboratory for placing abstract constraints into concrete computational objects and seeing which structures remain usable.
