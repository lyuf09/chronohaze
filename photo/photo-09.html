灰潮

17/09/2023

英国爱丁堡 · 2023年

这组照片拍摄于刚到爱丁堡的初秋。阴雨、海面、白色花朵、乌鸦与潮湿的石墙共同构成一种进入城市前后的临界感。灰暗、潮湿、带着轻微的不安。夹在其中的自拍也成为观看的一部分，像是在确认自己如何被这座城市的雨、风与暗光慢慢收纳。

This series was made in early autumn, shortly after I arrived in Edinburgh. Rain, the sea, white flowers, crows, wet stone, and a dim self-portrait come together as a threshold between arrival and belonging. Grey, damp, slightly unsettled, and quietly absorbing. The self-portrait becomes part of this act of looking, as if asking how the body is slowly taken in by the rain, wind, and low light of a new place.
