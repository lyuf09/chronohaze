参照系

2023 年 4 月 1 日

中国重庆市 · 2023年

这组照片从地面、轻轨站二层与机场旁小型飞行基地的飞机上展开，记录同一座城市在不同高度中的变形。道路、轨道、楼群与山体不再只是地点，而像被速度和距离重新切开的线条。它们共同指向一种悬置的观看：熟悉的重庆在上升、经过与俯瞰之间，变得陌生、抽象，也更接近记忆本身。

This series moves through three positions of looking: from the ground, from the second level of a light rail station, and from a small aircraft departing from a flight base near the airport. I was interested in how elevation, speed, and vibration alter the city’s surface. Roads, rails, buildings, and mountains stop appearing as stable landmarks; instead, they become lines, cuts, and blue-grey fragments of movement. The work is a study of suspended perception, where a familiar Chongqing becomes distant, abstract, and briefly unfamiliar through transit and height.
