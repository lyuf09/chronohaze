铁与蕨

2023 年 10 月 21 日

英国格拉斯哥 · 2023年

这组照片拍摄于格拉斯哥植物园的温室内部。玻璃穹顶、白色立柱与深绿植物共同构成一种被收纳的自然。植物既像自由生长，也像被分类、陈列和保存的标本。潮湿的光线、阴影与反射让空间介于花园、档案与梦境之间，呈现一种安静而略带异质感的观看。

This series was made inside the greenhouses of Glasgow Botanic Gardens. I was drawn to the tension between cultivated structure and organic excess: glass roofs, white columns, iron details, ferns, moss, and dark layers of foliage. The plants appear both alive and arranged, growing freely while also being preserved, framed, and quietly displayed. Through dim light, reflection, and enclosed humidity, the space becomes neither simply a garden nor an interior, but a suspended archive of living forms—somewhere between botanical study, memory, and dream.
