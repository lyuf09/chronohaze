隙光

2023 年 8 月 9 日

中国重庆市 · 2023年

这组照片拍摄于夏日清晨的室内。为了捕捉早上六七点进入房间的光线，我没有走远，只在自己的房间与隔壁客房之间移动。窗帘、墙面、家具与被光照亮的细节共同构成的一种安静。在有限范围内观察光如何改变日常物件，使房间短暂地变得陌生、清澈而柔软。

This series was made indoors on an early summer morning. I woke up around six or seven specifically to photograph the light entering my room and the guest room next door, without moving far from these familiar spaces. Curtains, walls, furniture, and small illuminated details become part of a quiet study of domestic light. The images observe how morning light alters ordinary objects, making the space briefly unfamiliar, clear, and soft.
