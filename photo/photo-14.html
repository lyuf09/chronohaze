美国纽约州伊萨卡 · 2025年

这组照片拍摄于康奈尔植物园，在一种将雨未雨的阴天里展开。树荫、水面、木结构与被植物遮蔽的小径共同构成潮湿而低亮度的空间。植物不再只是被观看的景观，而像慢慢吞没路径、光线和边界的存在。不同于可能大多数人爱对焦的花朵，我更多试图整理一种深绿色的、接近雨前的感知：安静、浓稠、略带封闭感，却仍有微弱的光从暗处渗出。

This series was made at Cornell Botanic Gardens under an overcast sky, in the suspended moment before rain. Tree shade, water, wooden structures, and plant-covered paths form a damp, low-lit space where the plants are no longer simply scenery to be observed, but a presence that slowly absorbs paths, light, and boundaries. Rather than focusing on the flowers that often attract attention, I was interested in gathering a deep-green, pre-rain perception: quiet, dense, slightly enclosed, yet still allowing a faint light to seep through the dark.
