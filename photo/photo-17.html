最后的常景

2026 年 5 月 18 日

美国伊萨卡 · 2026年

这组照片拍摄于伊萨卡已经开始显得像“最后几次普通散步”之一的下午。我没有去寻找地标，而是看向那些即将失去日常性的事物：路边的房屋、停车场、树影、橱窗，以及五月光线落在熟悉表面上的方式。

它们并不记录一次正式告别，而是在离开真正发生之前，观察普通景象怎样慢慢变成记忆。没有任何一处突然变得戏剧化；变化的是观看者与地点之间的距离。

This series was made during what had begun to feel like one of my last ordinary walks in Ithaca. I did not look for landmarks, but for things about to lose their everyday quality: roadside houses, parking lots, tree shadows, shop windows, and the way May light settled on familiar surfaces.

The photographs do not stage a formal farewell. They watch ordinary views begin to become memory before departure fully arrives. Nothing in the landscape suddenly turns dramatic; what changes is the distance between the viewer and the place.
