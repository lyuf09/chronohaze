你的记忆

05/04/2025

英国爱丁堡 · 2025年

这组照片拍摄于爱丁堡 Meadows 与主图书馆附近，把春天放在校园建筑与日常通行之间观看。草坪上不知由谁整理的小花园、道路旁的花枝、树影、玻璃立面、石墙与自行车共同构成一种介于生活和记忆之间的场景。对一段行走中残留情绪的整理：明亮、柔软，透明的不确定感。

This series was made around the Meadows and the area near the Main Library in Edinburgh, looking at spring through the overlap between campus architecture and everyday passage. A small garden arranged by an unknown hand on the lawn, blossoms along the road, tree shadows, glass facades, stone walls, and bicycles together form a scene suspended between daily life and memory. The images become an attempt to gather what remains from a walk: bright, soft, and filled with a translucent sense of uncertainty.
